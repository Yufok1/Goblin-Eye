Its original MIT copyright and license are preserved in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
Goblin Eye modifications are maintained in this repository; this is not an
official upstream release or a Blizzard-endorsed addon.

Changes include research/evidence presentation, full reply handling, per-chat
settings and supported model discovery, the Kilo adapter/research policy,
instance ownership and publishing improvements, and local Windows speech.
The portable Goblin Eye installer replaces upstream's standalone installer.
No Warcraft recordings or neural voice cloning assets are distributed.
