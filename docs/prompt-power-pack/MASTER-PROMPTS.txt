GOBLIN EYE PROMPT POWER PACK

28 copy-and-paste research prompts, plus follow-up controls.

QUICK START
The master document is a library: copy one prompt at a time, not the entire document.
Open one numbered .txt file and paste its whole contents into a Goblin Eye-connected agent. Every prompt is ready to send unchanged. It selects its research subject from conversation and saved context, or gives a supported overview if essential context is missing. You can use the same prompts with any of your connected providers; a plain unconnected chat cannot inspect your Goblin Eye database.

These are research requests, not claims about your current character or market. They do not run automatically, create scheduled jobs, or save companion notes/goals. They work best with reasonably fresh saved addon data. No outro or session wrap-up is required.

START WITH THESE
01: What should I do next?
03: Find connections I am missing
17: What am I overlooking in my bags and bank?
21: Show me an overlooked possibility
22: Opportunity lab
26: Can Goblin Eye answer this well yet?

EXAMPLE CHAINS
Gear: 26 -> 05 -> 06 -> 07 -> 09
Profession project: 16 -> 15 -> 17 -> 12
Exploration: 19 -> 21 -> 20
Economic hypothesis: 22 -> 13 -> manually measure a trial -> 14
Second opinion: paste an answer into 24 using another connected provider.

Run one prompt at a time. Choose follow-ups based on the result; these chains are suggestions, not an automatic batch. A blocker or missing prerequisite is a useful result.
