# Goblin Eye prompt power pack

**28 research prompts**, individual text files, a master document, and follow-up controls. These are copy-and-paste requests for a Goblin Eye-connected agent, not automatic jobs.

## Start here

1. Open a numbered text file and copy its contents into your connected agent.
2. Send it unchanged. No item names, zones, budgets or other custom fields are required.
3. The agent uses established conversation and saved context, chooses a research scope, and checks the evidence. Ambiguous identity stays unresolved rather than being guessed.
4. Use a follow-up command when a result deserves more investigation.

**The master document is a browsing library. Copy one prompt from it at a time; do not paste the entire document as one request.** No personal character or account data is embedded. No session wrap-up is needed.

## Six good starting points

- **01 — What should I do next?** Choose between progression, an economic angle, and discovery.
- **03 — Find connections I am missing.** Join useful facts across your character and the world.
- **17 — Inventory detective.** Investigate possessions you may be overlooking.
- **21 — Something unusual.** Find an interesting, checkable lead.
- **22 — Opportunity lab.** Generate hypotheses and try to disprove them.
- **26 — Trust check.** See whether the data can support your question before a broad research run.

## Suggested sequences

- **Gear:** 26 → 05 → 06 → 07 → 09.
- **Professions:** 16 → 15 → 17 → 12.
- **Exploration:** 19 → 21 → 20.
- **Economic experiments:** 22 → 13 → manually measure a trial → 14.
- **Second opinion:** use 24 to check a recommendation already in the conversation.

Choose each next step based on the result. A blocker is a reason to adjust the sequence. The prompts do not save notes, create goals, schedule work or act in the game. A plain chat without Goblin Eye tools cannot inspect your database.
